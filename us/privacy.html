Effective date: [Effective Date]

This Privacy Policy explains how [Operator Name] ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you play [Game Name] (the "Game") and use related services (together, the "Service"). It also explains the choices and rights you have.

Summary. The Game does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number. It asks for your birth year (and month, only if needed) once, to work out your age range; it keeps the age range, not your birth date. It uses a random installation ID created on your device, basic device information, gameplay records, and purchase records to run the Game, process purchases, prevent cheating, and understand how the Game is played. Our advertising partners receive your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads; you can limit this (Sections 5 and 9). You can delete your account in the Game at any time (Section 7).

1. This policy applies to the Game and the Service. It does not apply to third-party apps, websites, or services, including the app stores and ads shown in the Game, which have their own privacy policies.

2. We currently offer the Service in the Republic of Korea, Japan, and the United States. The Service is not offered in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland. This version of the policy is for users in the United States.

5. Advertising and Your Choices

1. The Game does not use web cookies. The advertising and analytics SDKs in the Game read your device's advertising ID, a resettable ID provided by your operating system.

2. Android. To reset or delete your advertising ID, or to opt out of personalized ads, go to your device's Settings, then Google (or Privacy), then Ads. Depending on your Android version, choose "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization."

3. iOS (when available). The Game will ask for permission before accessing your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

4. In the Game. You can turn off the sale or sharing of your information for interest-based ads in Settings, then Privacy, then turn off Personalized Ads (see Section 9.4).

5. You can learn more about interest-based advertising in apps and opt out with the DAA AppChoices app.

6. If you opt out, you can still play the Game and watch rewarded ads. You will still see ads, but they will not be personalized based on your activity across apps.

7. Deleting Your Account

1. In the Game. Go to Settings, then "Delete Account," and follow the instructions. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

2. Outside the Game. If you have already uninstalled the Game, request deletion at https://typebi.github.io/idle-rpg-legal/us/delete-account.html or by emailing [Contact Email] with your installation ID (shown in Settings). If you don't know your installation ID, send the order number of a purchase so we can find your account.

3. What is deleted. Your account, game data, backups, ad reward records, anti-cheat records, coupon redemption records, and personal mailbox items are deleted from our game server, and the Game's data and settings are removed from your device. Purchase records are kept as described in Section 6. Analytics records linked to your installation ID are also deleted; only aggregate statistics that cannot identify you remain. If our analytics server is temporarily unavailable, they are deleted as soon as it recovers.

4. Deleting your account also deletes any unused virtual items, including unused Diamonds (see our Terms of Service).
