[Game Name] Your Privacy Choices

How to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, and how to ask to see, correct, or delete it, for [Game Name] operated by [Operator Name]. For the full details, see our Privacy Policy.

1. Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

We do not sell personal information for money. However, the Game's advertising partners receive your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information, and ad views to show and measure interest-based ads, which may count as a "sale" or "sharing" under California and other state laws.

To opt out, use any of these:

1. In the Game (fastest). Go to Settings, then Privacy, and turn off Personalized Ads. The change applies right away. You will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests, and rewarded ads keep working the same way.

2. On your device. Open your device's Settings, then Google (or Privacy), then Ads, and choose "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization."

3. By email. Send [Contact Email] a message with the subject "Do Not Sell or Share" and your installation ID (shown in the Game under Settings, then Account, with a Copy button).

If your age range is 13 to 15, Personalized Ads start turned off, and we do not sell or share your information unless you turn them on yourself.

This website does not use cookies, trackers, or ads and does not collect personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of here. The choices above apply to the Game.

2. Requests to Know, Access, Correct, or Delete

1. Delete right away. In the Game, go to Settings, then "Delete Account." If you already uninstalled the Game, see the account deletion page.

2. Other requests. Email [Contact Email] with what you would like us to do (for example, send you a copy of your information or correct it) and your installation ID. If you no longer have it, send a purchase order number (the number starting with GPA. in your Google Play receipt email).

3. Verification. Because we do not know your name or email, we match the installation ID or order number with our records. We may ask for more details if needed to protect your account.

4. Authorized agents. Someone you authorize may make a request for you. We may ask for proof of your written permission and ask you to confirm your identity directly.

5. Timing. We respond within 45 days. If we need more time (up to another 45 days), we will tell you why.

6. Appeals. If we decline your request, reply to our decision or email [Contact Email] with the subject "Privacy Appeal" within 60 days.

We will not treat you differently for using these rights.
